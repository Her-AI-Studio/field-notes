Okay, let’s talk about hydrangeas! They’re beautiful, iconic flowering shrubs known for their vibrant, often pink or blue, sprays of blooms. They’re generally pretty adaptable, thriving in well-drained soil and partial shade.  They typically have a central stalk with multiple branches, and the blooms are produced from the undersides of the leaves.

Heard: Rock Pigeon (60% confidence)

**Locality:** newton massachusetts
**Weather:** evening at sundown cool and breezy
**Habitat:** front yard mulched bed
